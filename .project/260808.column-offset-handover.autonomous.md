# Column offset: what landed, what Roundtrip.v still needs

Continues `260808.column-offset.autonomous.md`, which estimated this work
before it started. The estimate was roughly right about the parser and
wrong in one useful direction: the descent lemmas cost less than feared,
because five of the six descend from idle.

## State of the branch

`theories/Parser.v` and `theories/Wf.v` compile. `theories/Roundtrip.v`
does not, so **the project does not build from this branch**. `main` is
untouched and green.

**The bug is fixed and verified.** `- - b` / `  - c` now parses as one
outer item holding a two-item inner list, matching djot.js and djoths.
Before: a one-item inner list that swallowed `- c`.

Landed, all closed under the global context:

- `step_fuel` carries `off`, the column its line starts at. Added at
  `open_list (off + indent_of l)` and the routing test
  `Nat.ltb (ls_indent ls) (off + indent_of l)`; grown by `consumed l rest`
  at each descent through a quote prefix or list marker.
- `step_at off`, with `step = step_at 0`; `step_at_shift`, `step_at_idle`.
- **`step_fuel_shift`** — moving a run `k` columns right moves every
  recorded column by `k` and changes nothing else. No hypotheses at all:
  not even about fences, since `PFence` records no column. This is the
  uniform shift the old `pad_state` could not express, and it covers
  nested lists.
- `parse_lines_at`, `parse_lines_at_shift`, `parse_lines_shift` — the same
  fact at the fold, where `finish` erases the shift entirely.
- **`step_pad`** — `step (p ++ l) st = step_at (String.length p) l st` for
  blank `p` and `pad_safe st`. Padding reduces to a change of column;
  `pad_inner_ok` and the nested-list exclusion are gone. `pad_safe`
  survives only for `PFence`, the genuine gap.
- The six descent lemmas: five wrap their result in
  `pad_state (consumed l rest)`; `step_quote_cont` takes its hypothesis at
  `step_at`. The four quote fold lemmas take `pad_state 2 inner` (or
  `pad_state (String.length pad + 2)`), discharged at every top-level call
  site by `pad_state k (PPara []) = PPara []`.
- `Wf.v`'s `step_fuel_wf` and `step_fuel_supported` generalize over `off`
  mechanically; the offset never enters the measure.

Deleted: `pad_nested_list_unshifted`, `pad_inner_ok`,
`pad_invisible_inner`. The first was documenting the bug — it is false
now, which is the confirmation the design note asked for.

## What Roundtrip.v needs

Only **8** call sites of the changed lemmas, not the wholesale rewrite the
earlier estimate implied. Work in this order; the build is a good guard at
each step.

1. **`step_idle_bullet_cont`** (first failure, ~line 947). It currently
   claims `step (bullet_cont ++ l) (PPara []) = step l (PPara [])`, which
   is now false: the padded line starts two columns right. Restate as

   ```coq
   step (bullet_cont ++ l) (PPara []) = step_at 2 l (PPara [])
   ```

   and prove it from `step_pad` plus `bullet_cont_blank`. Then
   `step_at_idle` converts it to
   `(fst (step l (PPara [])), pad_state 2 (snd (step l (PPara []))))`
   wherever a caller wants the column-0 form.

2. **`parse_cblock_pad`.** This is the lemma the whole offset design makes
   redundant in principle: it re-derives, per cblock constructor, that a
   whitespace pad does not change what a block parses to. `step_pad` says
   that in one line for any state. Try replacing its induction with a
   `parse_lines`-level corollary of `step_pad` and `parse_lines_at_shift`
   before migrating it constructor by constructor — if that works, most of
   the 145 lines go away rather than being ported.

3. **The `LSt 0` occurrences (39).** These are the top-level list at
   column 0 and stay correct for flat lists, which is everything currently
   proven. Do not generalize them to `LSt k` until step 4 needs it.

4. **Nested lists.** Once the above builds, relax
   `Render.list_content_safe` to still exclude `CCode` but admit `CList`,
   and generalize the list-run lemmas from `LSt 0` to `LSt k`. The
   indent-shift argument that
   `260807.list-roundtrip-indent-shift.autonomous.md` called a separate
   proof project is now just `step_fuel_shift`.

## Things that will bite

- `consumed (p ++ l) rest = String.length p + consumed l rest` needs
  `String.length rest <= String.length l`; natural subtraction truncates.
  `classify_quote_length` / `classify_list_length` supply it.
- `0 + indent_of l` does not reduce on its own in a goal built by `cbn
  [step_fuel]`. `rewrite !Nat.add_0_l` before `rewrite Hind`.
- Descent offsets come out as `off + String.length p + c` where the goal
  wants `String.length p + off + c`; `Nat.add_comm` on those two.
- `rocq-mcp` sessions can hold a stale `Strings.vo` and claim
  `length_append` does not exist. Trust `dune build` over the session.
