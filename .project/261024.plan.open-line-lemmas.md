# Plan: the remaining `step` proofs go through `open_line`

## Goal

Five inductions over `Step.step_fuel` unfold `open_line` into every
branch that opens a line, and repeat the opening cases there: idle,
paragraph interrupt, and each container's close-and-reopen.  The other
five already state one `open_line_*` lemma and apply it.  Bring the five
to the same form, so a change to how a line opens is proved once per
property instead of at every branch that opens one.

hypothesis and does the three descending kinds once.

## Why this and not more

Measured during the same audit and left out:

- **A generic invariant or simulation principle for `step_fuel`.**  The
  ten proofs do not share one shape: five are unary invariants with
  different helper lemmas, three commute with a state map, and two
  (`pad`, `stable`) change the input or the fuel.  The proofs that
  already go through `open_line_*` and `close_reopen_*` are the shortest
  of their kind, which is what a principle would buy.  Revisit if a new
  invariant over `step_fuel` would be the sixth of one shape.
- **Merging `PParaOff` into `PPara`.**  Its branch in `step_fuel` repeats
  the paragraph branch, but `PParaOff` appears 31 times and `PPara` 918
  times in `theories/` and `dev/`.  The merge moves far more than it
  removes.
- **Making `step_fuel` opaque outside `Step.v`.**  `Uniformity.v`,
  `ListUniformity.v`, `Tightness.v` and `Reached.v` unfold it about 115
  times beside about 200 uses of the `step_*` equation lemmas.  Closing
  that needs equation lemmas for every unfolded branch.  The one ripple
  inspected (`b5f88a1`, a blank ends an open spec) changed theorem
  statements as well as proofs, which opacity would not have saved.
- **Splitting `InlinePrecedence.v`** (9482 lines).  It is already in 28
  headed sections in reading order; separate files would turn `Local`
  lemmas into exported ones for no gain in structure.

## Is it worth it now

`open_line` and the opening helpers changed in about twenty commits from
2026-08-23 to 2026-09-28, each touching `Wf.v` and `Step.v` proofs for
30 to 1089 lines, and in none since.  The payoff depends on new line
kinds: the block rows marked "new" or "widen" in
[[dialect-extensions]] (`line_blocks`, `example_lists`, pipe tables
without outer bars, pandoc's `::: {#id}` opener, fence attributes) would
each add or change an opening case.  If none of those is planned, this
plan can wait.
