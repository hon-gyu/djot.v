# Proposal: finish the inline scanner's source accumulators

## Problem and diagnosis

The scanner still grows source strings with `acc ++ one c` in escaped
whitespace, verbatim, destination, raw-spec, span/attribute, reference/note, wiki, autolink,
and symbol states. Each construct can cost quadratic time in its length.
Adjacent `Str` nodes can also be merged with `t ++ s` by `isnoc` and
`osnoc`.

The destination measurement needs a qualification. At `16e4b24`, a
throwaway executable timed a single `[x](aaaa...)` line in the dev
profile. It separately timed (a) feeding its ordinary-reading shadow
through the generic step, (b) repeated one-byte string appends, (c)
`drop_nl` on the completed destination, and (d) closing a constructed
`IDest` state. Best of three, milliseconds:

| destination bytes | full scan | shadow | appends | `drop_nl` | close |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 20,000 | 89.5 | 2.0 | 11.7 | 65.3 | 67.3 |
| 40,000 | 448.7 | 5.4 | 44.5 | 397.9 | 385.0 |
| 80,000 | 2269.4 | 9.4 | 154.7 | 2449.5 | 2465.6 |

These independently timed parts are diagnostic, not an additive cost
model; GC state differs between runs. `drop_nl` dominates. It matches
`String c rest` once per byte, and native-string extraction copies each
tail. The shadow is near linear, and the close adds little beyond
`drop_nl`; `bnode` is not the cause of the steep growth here. The
diagnostic executable was removed after measurement.

A second baseline probe used `convert --time 1` for semantic block parse
of one construct per line. For raw-format spec `` `x`{=aaaa...} ``, times
at 2, 4, and 8 KB of spec were 53, 651, and 7666 ms. In
`iraw_step_at`, `vstop := spot_before cursor_start (String lbrace spec)`
is computed before the branch, on every byte. `spot_before` structurally
walks the growing string, copying native-string tails. It is needed
only on exit. Moving it into those exit branches is the first fix for
this shape, before changing its accumulator representation. Other
single-construct probes also grew superlinearly over 2 to 8 KB:
verbatim 0.7 to 4.7 ms; reference 0.6 to 7.3; note 0.9 to 21.1;
wiki 0.5 to 7.4; autolink 0.7 to 16.2; symbol 0.6 to 9.0;
attribute 0.9 to 17.5; span 0.6 to 9.4. These are coarse,
single-run rankings, not before/after evidence.

## Intended result

Appending source to the remaining scanner fields costs time proportional
to the appended piece. Source is joined only when a construct closes,
fails, or a predicate needs its value. The semantic and located parser
results, including source spans, remain identical. There are no admitted
lemmas or new axioms.

For destination performance, also make `drop_nl` a linear scan in the
native execution path. Without that, a faster `dst` accumulator barely
changes the measured line even though it removes one quadratic cost.

## Design choices for review

1. **Buffer type.** Prefer the existing `Buf` and `TextOps` for source
   fields. The string instance remains the specification. The `chunks`
   instance avoids prefix copies. `tval` runs at the existing decision
   points, such as `auto_body_ok` and `raw_spec_ok`, and at close or
   fallback. A second type parameter would separate source and pending
   text conceptually, but doubles the scanner's type and simulation
   surface without an identified execution benefit.
2. **Native batching.** First change the buffer representation and prove
   the simulation. Then measure long verbatim and destination lines. If
   per-byte calls remain material, batch plain runs in those two native
   driver arms and check the trusted realization by differential test.
3. **Scope.** First move raw-spec position computation to the exit
   branches and address `drop_nl` at destination close. Then change the
   fields whose source can run for a full line: `IEscWs`, `IVerb`,
   `IDest`, and `IRaw`. Continue with the other named source fields, giving each
   changed helper a commuting lemma before
   changing the main step proof. Keep `isnoc`/`osnoc` separate because
   their output is already flat AST strings, outside `iscan_g`'s buffer
   representation. Measure their cost and change them only with a clean
   representation or join strategy; do not obscure the scanner proof
   with an unproven AST rewrite.

## Checkpoints

1. Move `iraw_step_at`'s `spot_before` call into its exit branches.
   Make destination newline removal linear under extraction, with a
   Gallina equality proof or a clearly isolated trusted substitution
   and differential check. Measure the two changes independently.
2. Change `IEscWs`, `IVerb`, `IDest`, and `IRaw` to `Buf`; update `map_text` and
   `lift`, helper lemmas, and the step/break/finish simulation. Compile
   the touched theories without admits or new axioms.
3. Change the remaining source fields, grouping by the helper that
   consumes them: attribute source, labels, wiki region,
   autolink source, and symbol alias. Preserve predicate timing and
   fallback behavior. Add focused shapes to `test/bench.ml`.
4. Compare old and new extraction in one process on exhaustive short
   strings, random strings, and long targeted constructs. Confirm the
   harness catches a planted difference. Run the repository checks in
   the handover, regenerate `dist/`, and test the standalone package.
5. Time both extractions in one process on the changed constructs and
   on `line`, `paragraph`, `brackets`, `emphasis`, and `links`. Record
   the dated results in `extracted-performance.md`, then commit.

## Limits

Structural string matches elsewhere in `Line`, `Strings`, `Attributes`,
`Marker`, `str_last`, and `wiki_split` can still copy tails. This change
accounts for them when interpreting benchmarks but does not imply they
are fixed. A buffer append can become linear while a close-time
predicate or helper remains quadratic; each long construct therefore
needs its own end-to-end measurement.

## Result

The single `Buf` parameter now covers every source field named above.
The raw-spec position calculation runs only on exit, and `drop_nl`
has a native linear realization. The existing simulation proofs and
downstream located, inversion, and well formedness proofs compile.
No native batching was added: verbatim and destination now scale near
linearly at 20 to 80 KB. The old/new differential, corpus, roundtrip,
located, distribution, and benchmark results are recorded in
`extracted-performance.md`.

`isnoc` and `osnoc` still merge flat AST strings. Wrapped brackets and
emphasis scale near linearly, so this change leaves their representation
alone. The helper-path costs in `source_shape`, `wiki_split`, and other
structural string scans remain open and dominate several whole-construct
benchmarks even though their source appends are now cheap.
