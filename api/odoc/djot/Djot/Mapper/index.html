val map_doc : t -> Doc.t -> Doc.t

The document made of the mapped blocks, as Doc.make gives it, with the profile and the positions of the one given. The mapper sees Doc.blocks without the identifiers in Doc.auto_identifiers; they are derived again from the result, and so are the sections, Doc.footnotes, Doc.references and Doc.auto_references.

The result is not tied to any text: an edit of the Source the document came from gives the parse of the edited text, without the map.
